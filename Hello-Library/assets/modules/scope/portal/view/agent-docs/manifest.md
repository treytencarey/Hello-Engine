# scope/portal/view

How a portal looks on a client, and the world it shows. A placed portal asks root to observe its target. Every portal asks to start the target when nobody runs it: one this client sees being placed, a door (a portal that arrives with its scene, as a map's door from Blender through `gltf/mod_registry`, or one a console points somewhere), and one placed before this client came (an invite's portal, a portal a late joiner finds). So a world comes up when a portal to it appears or gets its target, and an ask taken away (the target or the portal gone) lets it go. Starting needs a player who may start the world (the Gateway's start gate); anyone else's ask falls back to join-only (core's `core/world/load` ladder), and the portal stays locked `not-running` for them. Root's answer decides: allowed, the target's picture shows through the portal's mesh, drawn from the pose of this player's `camera/3d/eye` (core's pose bridge); refused, the portal is locked and says why. Over each placed portal that shows another world stands its label, facing this client's eye: the world's repository name and folder, its branch, and how many players are in it, by root's sessions list. Client only (`client/init.lua`); on a server the component is plain data.

## Components

- `scope/portal/view = { mode }`: `"3d"`, the view brings a sphere of radius 1.2; `"none"`, the carrier brings its own mesh (a glTF door's surface), and the view only puts the stencil on it.
- What it writes: the observe ask (below), `core/compositor/stencil { world = target }` on the mesh that shows the target, `scope/portal/locked { reason }` on the portal, and for `"3d"` the portal's `Mesh3d` and `MeshMaterial3d<StandardMaterial>`.
- A door (a portal with `scope/portal.at`, on a node with a `Name`) shows its target from the place `at` names there: its stencils are `core/compositor/stencil { world = target, at, frame, view }`, with `frame` the door's world transform (the carrier's `GlobalTransform:compute_transform()`, as plain `{ translation, rotation, scale }`) and `view` the door's `Name`, unique in its map. Core publishes the eye relative to `frame`, and the target places that view at the node `at` names there, one picture per door (`core/compositor`). A portal without `at` writes none of the three, and shows the target as it stands, the identity view.
- The ask, one per placed portal, a child of it: `{ ["core/world/observe"] = { ref = target, libraries = <this world's library records>, hosted = <true while scope/portal/hosted>, join_only = false }, WorldShared = {}, ["core/net/sync"] = false, ["core/script/owner"] = false }`. `libraries` holds copies of this world's own `core/world/library` records (not mirrors), each `{ library, cache, order }`, so the observer world reads the viewer's libraries. Root answers on its own entity, `core/world/observe/answer { ref, state, reason }`.
- `scope/portal/view/label = { target }`: the label, one per placed portal that shows another world (its target, or a door's way back, as the ask's), a child of the portal: `{ ["scope/portal/view/label"] = { target }, Mesh3d = <Plane3d facing +Z, 4.8 x 0.2 m>, ["ui/screen"] = { width = 1440, height = 60, transparent = true }, Transform = {}, ["transform/follow"] = { rotation, offset }, Visibility = "Visible", ["core/net/sync"] = false }`. `transform/follow` keeps it over the portal: `offset` 0.5 m over the top of the portal's bounds (its own `Aabb` and its mesh children's, at their `Transform`, the label left out; 1.2 m for a `"3d"` portal and 2 m for a door until they are known), and `rotation` the turn about Y that faces the plane to this client's `camera/3d/eye`. Its child is its sessions ask, `{ ["core/registry/sessions"] = {}, Request = {} }`: root keeps one list however many ask, and the ask goes with its label. Neither is kept through a reload: the reload takes them and the next run spawns them again.
- `scope/portal/view/line = { part }`: the label's text, under its screen's root (`ui/screen/root`, found by `UiTargetCamera` naming the screen's camera), each in a layer of its own: a `Node` filling the root, absolute, centring its text, neither blocking nor hovering. `"text"`: the one line, 34 px, never wrapped (`TextLayout` `NoWrap`), `ui/style` `text`: the target's repository name and folder (`Other-World`, `Hello-Engine/Hello`), `"  |  "`, the branch, `"  |  "` and the count from root's sessions reply, the session whose scope matches the target's in canonical form (owner and name lowered): `"1 player"`, `"<n> players"` (its `active`), or `"no one here"` when the list names no session for it (as a world this PC holds only locally, not on the registry). Until root answers, or while its reply carries an `error`, the line ends at the branch. A target not in the canonical form is shown as it is. `"outline"`: eight copies of the line in black at 0.85 alpha, their layers set off 2 px each way and diagonally, spawned before the text's so they draw under it. The root has no background, and the screen is transparent. A changed line bumps the screen's `fresh`, since a transparent screen is never cleared.
- A door's surfaces (its mesh children) never include its label: the stencil is never written on it.

## Systems

| Schedule | Set | Purpose |
|----------|-------|---------|
| Update | `scope/portal/view/client` | Observe: each placed portal whose target (for a door without one, this client's player's way back through it) is not this world's scope keeps one ask naming its target; an ask naming another ref (a changed target) or a second one is despawned, and `hosted` is set while the portal has `scope/portal/hosted` and taken out when it goes (`entry("core/world/observe"):and_modify`, so the ask stays and root sees it change). Every ask, kept or new, carries `join_only = false`. Runs on `scope/portal`, `scope/portal/view` or `scope/portal/hosted` added, `scope/portal` changed, `placement` or `scope/portal/hosted` removed, and this world's own row changing (a promoted world catches up) |
| Update | `scope/portal/view/client` | Settle: from root's answers only (`WorldMirror.world_id == 0`): `allowed` puts `core/compositor/stencil { world = target }` on the portal's mesh, a door's with its `at`, `frame` and `view`, and removes `scope/portal/locked`; `refused` removes the stencil and sets `scope/portal/locked { reason }` (`refused` when none is given). With `"none"` and no `Mesh3d` on the carrier, the mesh is each child with a `Mesh3d` (a glTF node's primitives). The stencil is inserted whole, which drops the `ref` and `vm` core wrote on it, so it is written only when its `world`, `at`, `view` or `frame` differs (the frame to 1e-4). An answer about this world's own scope (another world's portal looking here) does nothing to a portal leading here. Runs on an answer added or changed, `scope/portal/view` or `Mesh3d` added, `placement` removed, a placed viewed portal's `scope/portal` or `GlobalTransform` changed (a door moved, or read before its first propagation as the identity), and this world's own row changing |
| PostUpdate | `scope/portal/view/client` | Look, `"3d"` only, and not for a portal to this world's own scope: on `scope/portal/view` or `scope/portal/locked` added, the lock changed, or `placement` or the lock removed, give the portal a sphere `Mesh3d` of radius 1.2 if it has none, and a material: red and glowing while being placed (base 1.0, 0.3, 0.1; emissive 2.0, 0.5, 0.1), dim while locked (0.15, 0.15, 0.18), plain while waiting; nothing while the stencil is on, as the stencil owns the material then |
| Update | `scope/portal/view/client` | On `scope/portal/view` removed from an entity that stays: remove the stencil (from the carrier and its mesh children) and the lock, the sphere and its material for `"3d"`, and despawn the portal's asks |
| Update | `scope/portal/view/label` | Label: each placed portal whose shown target is not this world's scope keeps one label naming that target, with its sessions ask; a label naming another target, or a second one, is despawned. Runs on the observe system's edges, but not `scope/portal/hosted`'s |
| Update | `scope/portal/view/label_face` | Every frame, as a camera follows: write each label's `transform/follow` offset and rotation (above) when they differ (offset to 1e-3, rotation to 1e-4) |
| Update | `scope/portal/view/label_text` | On `ui/screen/root` or a label added, or a label or a sessions reply changed: a label's screen root with no lines gets its outline and its line; one with them has their text set when it differs, and its screen a fresh image |

None of these run in a world that only observes (its own `core/world/status` says `observer = true`); a promoted world's row changes, which runs them over every portal it skipped.

## Use

`git/panel`'s server half spawns a player's portal with `scope/portal/view { mode = "3d" }`. A scene door names `"none"`:

```json
{ "scope/portal": { "target": "github:<owner>/<repository>:new-room" },
  "scope/portal/view": { "mode": "none" } }
```

A door that shows its target from a node of the target's map, here one named `arrival`:

```json
{ "scope/portal": { "target": "github:<owner>/<repository>:new-room", "at": "arrival" },
  "scope/portal/view": { "mode": "none" } }
```

## Notes

- A portal without `target` leads nowhere: it asks root nothing and shows nothing, and when its target is taken away its ask, its stencils and its lock go.
- A door without `target` that this client's own player came out of (its `scope/portal/from.door` is the door's `Name`) shows the world the player came from (`from.target`), from the door's own place there, as the crossing would take the player. Another player's way back shows nothing here. It follows the player's `scope/portal/from` arriving, changing or going, and a door spawned again with its scene.
- A portal to this world's own scope shows nothing: it asks nothing, takes no stencil and gets no look. That is a two-ended portal's return end in the portal's own world, which stands where the portal does (`scope/portal`).
- Only root's answer counts: a world's own entity or another world's mirror could say anything. `core/world/load.hosted` is root's own; this mod never writes it.
- Root asks once more after a `not-running` refusal when the ask carries `hosted` (core's `core/world/observe`); a second refusal is final until `hosted` goes and comes back.
- A started target stays up while any portal looks at it: root's observer world counts as a connection in the target's row, and the Gateway tears a world down only after it has had no connections for its grace. When the last ask goes, root releases it and the target idles out.
- The ask is not reaped with this mod's file, so a reload keeps it and root keeps the observed world loaded. It goes with its portal, with the view, or when the target changes.
- The stencil swaps the mesh's material and, when it goes, leaves none (`core/compositor/stencil`). A `"none"` door that is allowed and then refused shows no surface until its scene respawns; a `"3d"` portal gets the dim material.
- Suite: `tests/test_view.lua`, headless; a portal to this world's own scope asks nothing and takes no stencil from an answer about this world, and a portal seen being placed, a door under a real glTF scene and a portal placed before all ask to start their world; a door's stencils carry its `at`, its turned frame and its view, a portal without `at` writes none of them, and the door moved alone, its stencils' frame follows; a door without `target` asks nothing, given one asks and shows it, and with it taken away again its ask and stencils go; a door without `target` that this client's player came out of, after its scene was written again, shows the world the player came from, from the door's place, another player's way back shows nothing, and the way back gone, nothing. The look (sphere and colours) needs StandardMaterial assets, which a headless run lacks, and has no case, so no case shows that a portal to this world gets no sphere either. `tests/test_view_label.lua`, headless, a client world (`world_id = 7`) with root's sessions reply and observe answer stood in: one label per placed portal to another world, none for this world's own scope or one being placed; the text before and after root's list (the count matched in canonical form, none for a world it doesn't name); a 3d portal's label 1.7 m up, a door's over its mesh children, facing the eye and turning when it moves; a door's stencil on its surface and never on its label; a changed target's label names the new one; the target taken away, the label and its ask go; one line over eight outline copies, on a transparent screen with no background, and a fresh image when the line changes. The label's colours, and what a transparent screen shows, need a theme and a window, and have no case.
